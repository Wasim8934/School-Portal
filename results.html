Simple Process

How to Check Your Result

Follow these simple steps to view your exam result online in seconds.

1

Enter Roll Number

Type your Roll Number or Registration ID provided by the school in the search box above.

2

Click Search

Press the Search button or hit Enter to look up your result in the school database.

3

View Result

Your subject-wise marks, grades, total score, percentage and pass/fail status will appear.

4

Print DMC

Click "Print DMC" to generate and print your official Detailed Marks Certificate directly.

Result Information

  • Results are published after each exam term (Mid-Term & Final).
  • Roll Numbers are issued at the start of each academic year.
  • Original DMC can be collected from the school office.
  • Supply exams are held within 30 days of result declaration.

Need Help?

If you cannot find your result or have any queries, please contact the school administration office directly.
