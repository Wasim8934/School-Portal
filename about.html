About Azan Public School

Home / About Us

Established 2008

Serving Quality Education

Our Legacy

Committed to Quality, Character, and Community

Founded under the academic leadership of Principal Wasim Fida (BSCS), Azan Public School (located at Ladon, Peer Adil, Dera Ghazi Khan) stands as a benchmark of academic distinction, computer science awareness, and moral uprightness. We provide an environment where children are encouraged to question, experiment, and develop a genuine passion for learning.

Our educational framework fuses standard national board requirements with modern analytical methodologies. From early childhood phonics to secondary physics laboratories, our pedagogy is continuously updated to reflect modern best practices.

Holistic Development

Balancing intellect, physical fitness, and moral consciousness.

Individual Mentorship

Optimal student-to-teacher ratio ensuring personal focus.

Purpose & Direction

Our Vision, Mission & Values

Our Vision

To be a nationally celebrated institute of educational excellence that produces visionary, ethically sound, and scientifically adept leaders equipped to meet 21st-century global challenges.

Our Mission

To foster a secure, inclusive, and mentally stimulating environment where pupils acquire conceptual mastery, critical thinking habits, high moral ideals, and civic responsibility.

Core Values

  • Integrity (Diyanat): Truthfulness and uprightness in all deeds.
  • Excellence (Kamal): Striving for peak performance consistently.
  • Respect (Adab): Treating teachers, peers, and parents with honor.
  • Compassion: Cultivating kindness and mutual empathy.
Infrastructure

World-Class Campus Facilities

Designed to give our pupils the best space to study, discover, and excel.

Advanced Computer Laboratories

Equipped with modern desktop computers, high-speed fiber internet, and programming software for coding and ICT curricula.

Dedicated Science Labs

Separate, fully ventilated and standardized labs for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology with high-grade apparatus and safety equipment.

Extensive Knowledge Library

Over 5,000 titles including encyclopedias, Islamic literature, science reference books, classic novels, and academic periodicals.

Smart Multimedia Classrooms

Interactive projectors and multimedia visual aids bringing complex academic topics to life with immersive digital content.

First Aid & Health Clinic

On-campus first-aid clinic with trained medical staff to provide instant care and regular seasonal health check-ups.

Hygienic School Cafeteria

Clean, healthy food prepared in sanitary conditions providing nutritious snacks, fresh juices, and hot meals for students.
