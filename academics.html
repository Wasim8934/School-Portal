Academic Wings & Curriculum

Home / Academics
Ages 3 - 5 Years

Pre-School & Montessori Wing

Our Early Childhood section nurtures joyful curiosity through child-centered Montessori apparatus, phonics-based language development, sensorial refinement, and foundational numeracy.

  • Playgroup, Nursery, and Kindergarten (Prep) classes
  • Jolly Phonics reading and speech articulation techniques
  • Colorful, carpeted child-safe classrooms with dedicated play area
  • Basic Islamic supplications (Dua), etiquettes, and hygiene training
Enroll in Montessori

Montessori Activities

Sensory games, motor skills, storytelling, color perception, and social interaction.

Primary Education

Laying the strongest foundation in language, mathematics, and inquiry-based science.

Grades 1 - 5

Primary School Wing

In the Primary Section, children transition to structured academic inquiry. We place special focus on English communication, mathematical reasoning, conceptual science, and expressive Urdu writing.

  • Oxford & Single National Curriculum aligned textbooks
  • Introduction to Computer Hardware, Software, and Digital Literacy
  • Quran Nazra with proper Tajweed rules by trained Qaris
  • Regular arts, handwriting, mental math, and spelling bee contests
Enroll in Primary
Grades 6 - 8

Middle School Wing

Middle school serves as the vital bridge between elementary learning and high-stakes board examinations. We encourage deeper subject specialization, independent research projects, and scientific investigation.

  • Separate classes and dedicated discipline for boys and girls
  • Hands-on weekly experiments in Science Laboratories
  • Computer curriculum including Scratch, basic Python, and Web concepts
  • Pre-Matric test series ensuring smooth transition to Grade 9
Enroll in Middle School

Scientific Curiosity

Encouraging logic, analytical thinking, debate, and problem-solving skills.

Matric Board Excellence

Outstanding board positions and 100% passing rates in Federal and Rawalpindi boards.

Grades 9 - 10

Secondary & Matriculation (BISE)

Our secondary section is renowned for outstanding board results. With rigorously structured syllabus completion schedules, frequent chapter-wise assessments, and comprehensive pre-board revision sessions, we ensure students secure top grades to enter prestigious medical, engineering, and IT colleges.

Available Groups:

  • Biology Group (Science): Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics
  • Computer Science Group: Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science, Mathematics
