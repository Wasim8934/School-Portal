Simple 4-Step Process

How to Secure Admission

We follow a transparent and straightforward admission procedure designed for your convenience.

1

Online Application

Fill out the registration form below or obtain a physical form from the school counter.

2

Assessment / Interview

Child appears for an age-appropriate written test (English, Urdu, Math) or informal conversation.

3

Offer of Admission

Successful candidates receive an official offer letter and fee challan within 48 hours.

4

Fee Deposit & Kit

Deposit the admission fee voucher and collect the student ID, syllabus booklist, and uniform code.

Required Documents for Admission

  • 3 Passport-sized recent photographs with blue background.
  • Copy of Student's NADRA B-Form or Birth Certificate.
  • Copy of Father's / Guardian's Computerized CNIC.
  • Original School Leaving Certificate (SLC) from previous school (Class 1 onwards).
  • Copy of the latest Academic Report Card / Result Sheet.

Merit Scholarships & Kinship Discount

Azan Public School offers up to 50% tuition waiver for students securing 90%+ marks in board examinations, as well as 15% concession for the second and third siblings.
