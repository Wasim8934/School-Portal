Welcome to Azan Public School

Message from the Principal

Principal: Wasim Fida (BSCS)

It is my distinct honor to welcome you to Azan Public School, located at Ladon, Peer Adil, Dera Ghazi Khan. We believe that true education goes far beyond memorizing facts and scoring marks; it is the comprehensive development of a child's character, intellect, curiosity, and moral integrity.

With modern computing technology and science education at our core, Azan Public School ensures high academic rigor, well-equipped computer education, science awareness, alongside interactive classrooms. Simultaneously, we emphasize Islamic values, adab (respect), and leadership skills.

Whether your child is taking their first steps in our joyful Montessori wing or preparing for BISE Board examinations, our experienced faculty ensures personal guidance at every step of their journey.

Wasim Fida (BSCS)
Principal, Azan Public School
